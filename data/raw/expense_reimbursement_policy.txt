EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Purpose

This policy defines requirements for submitting, approving, and reimbursing legitimate business expenses incurred by employees.

1. Eligible Business Expenses

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable and necessary expenses incurred while performing authorized company business.

Expenses must have a legitimate business purpose and comply with applicable company guidelines.

2. Prior Approval

Certain expenses, including significant purchases and business travel, may require manager approval before the expense is incurred.

Employees should confirm approval requirements before making commitments on behalf of the company.

3. Receipts and Documentation

Employees must provide appropriate receipts or supporting documentation for reimbursable expenses when required.

Expense submissions should clearly identify the business purpose, date, amount, and relevant project or activity.

4. Expense Submission

Reimbursement requests must be submitted through the company's approved expense-management process.

Employees should submit expenses promptly after they are incurred.

Incomplete or inaccurate expense reports may be returned for correction and may delay reimbursement.

5. Travel Expenses

Reasonable transportation, accommodation, and other approved travel costs may be reimbursed when travel is authorized for business purposes.

Employees are expected to select reasonable travel options consistent with business requirements.

6. Meals

Reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved business travel or authorized business activities may be reimbursable.

Personal meals unrelated to approved business activities are not reimbursable.

7. Personal Expenses

Personal purchases and expenses unrelated to company business are not eligible for reimbursement.

Employees must separate personal expenses from business expenses when possible.

8. Corporate Payment Methods

When company-issued payment methods are provided, employees must use them only for authorized business purposes.

Loss, theft, or suspected unauthorized use of a company payment card must be reported promptly.

9. Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for reviewing expense requests for accuracy, appropriate documentation, business purpose, and compliance with company requirements.

Approval of an expense does not override applicable company policy.

10. False or Misleading Claims

Employees must not knowingly submit false, duplicate, altered, or misleading reimbursement claims.

Suspected fraudulent expense activity may be investigated according to applicable company procedures.

11. Record Retention

Expense documentation must be retained according to applicable financial-record and company retention requirements.

12. Compliance

Failure to follow expense-reimbursement requirements may result in delayed or denied reimbursement and further action under applicable company policies.
