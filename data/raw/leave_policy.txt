LEAVE AND TIME-OFF POLICY

Purpose

This policy defines employee leave categories, request procedures, approvals, and responsibilities related to time away from work.

1. Annual Leave

Eligible employees receive annual leave according to their employment terms and applicable company guidelines.

Annual leave should normally be requested in advance through the approved leave-management system.

Managers may approve or decline requested dates based on staffing requirements, project commitments, and business needs.

2. Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Employees must follow applicable company procedures when recording sick leave.

Supporting documentation may be required for extended absences according to company guidelines and applicable law.

3. Emergency Leave

Employees may request emergency leave when unexpected personal circumstances require immediate time away from work.

The employee should notify their manager as soon as possible and provide relevant information when appropriate.

4. Planned Leave

Employees should submit planned leave requests early enough to allow managers to arrange workload coverage.

Employees should avoid making non-refundable arrangements before required leave approval has been received.

5. Extended Leave

Requests for extended periods of leave may require additional approval from management or Human Resources.

Employees should discuss extended leave requirements with Human Resources as early as reasonably possible.

6. Leave Approval

Submission of a leave request does not automatically mean that the leave has been approved.

Employees should confirm approval through the company's approved leave-management process before beginning planned leave.

7. Handover Responsibilities

Before planned leave, employees should communicate important deadlines, responsibilities, and outstanding work to their manager or designated colleague.

Access credentials and passwords must never be shared as part of a work handover.

8. Returning to Work

Employees returning from extended leave should coordinate with their manager regarding project updates, responsibilities, and any required access changes.

9. Confidentiality

Medical or personal information provided in connection with a leave request must be handled according to applicable privacy requirements and company procedures.

10. Compliance

Employees and managers are responsible for following applicable leave procedures and maintaining accurate leave records.
