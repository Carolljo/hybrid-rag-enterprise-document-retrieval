PASSWORD AND ACCESS CONTROL POLICY

Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for passwords, authentication, user accounts, and access to company systems.

1. Password Requirements

Employees must use strong, unique passwords for company accounts.

Passwords must not be shared with coworkers, managers, contractors, or other unauthorized individuals.

Employees must not reuse company passwords for personal accounts or external services.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for company systems that support it.

Authentication codes, recovery codes, and security tokens must be protected and must not be shared with another person.

Unexpected MFA requests must be denied and reported to the IT security team.

3. Account Access

Employees must use only accounts assigned to them.

Shared accounts are prohibited unless specifically approved for a documented business requirement.

Access permissions must be limited to the systems and information required for an employee's job responsibilities.

4. Privileged Access

Administrative and privileged accounts must only be used for authorized administrative activities.

Privileged credentials must receive additional protection and must not be used for routine activities such as email or general web browsing.

5. Remote Access

Remote access to restricted internal systems must use the company-approved virtual private network (VPN).

Employees must complete required authentication, including MFA where supported, before accessing protected company resources remotely.

6. Credential Security

Passwords and authentication credentials must not be stored in unencrypted documents, spreadsheets, messages, or personal note-taking applications.

Employees who suspect that a password or authentication credential has been compromised must change the affected credential and report the incident immediately.

7. Access Reviews

User access permissions must be reviewed periodically.

Access that is no longer required because of a role change, project completion, or employment termination must be removed promptly.

8. Account Lockout and Suspicious Activity

Repeated unsuccessful login attempts may result in temporary account lockout.

Employees must report suspicious login notifications, unauthorized account activity, or unexpected password-reset requests to the IT security team.

9. Compliance

Failure to follow password and access-control requirements may result in suspension of system access and further action under applicable company policies.
