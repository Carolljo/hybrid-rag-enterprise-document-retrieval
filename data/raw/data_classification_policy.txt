DATA CLASSIFICATION POLICY

Purpose

This policy defines how company information must be classified, handled, stored, transmitted, and protected according to its sensitivity.

1. Classification Levels

Company information is classified into four levels:

Public — Information approved for public distribution.

Internal — Information intended for employees and authorized business partners.

Confidential — Sensitive business information that could cause harm if disclosed without authorization.

Restricted — Highly sensitive information requiring the strongest access and security controls.

2. Public Information

Public information may be shared externally after it has been approved for public distribution.

Examples may include published marketing materials, public product documentation, and approved press releases.

3. Internal Information

Internal information must only be shared with employees or authorized parties who have a legitimate business need.

Employees must use approved company systems when storing or transmitting internal information.

4. Confidential Information

Confidential information must only be accessed by individuals with an authorized business need.

Confidential information must be stored using approved company storage systems and transmitted using approved secure communication channels.

Employees must take precautions to prevent unauthorized individuals from viewing confidential information.

5. Restricted Information

Restricted information requires the highest level of protection.

Access must be limited according to the principle of least privilege.

When restricted information is accessed remotely, employees must use the company-approved virtual private network (VPN) and required authentication controls.

Restricted information must not be stored on personal or unmanaged devices.

6. Data Transmission

Confidential and restricted information must not be transmitted through personal email accounts, unapproved messaging applications, or unauthorized file-sharing services.

Approved encrypted communication and file-transfer systems must be used where required.

7. Data Storage

Sensitive information must be stored only in approved locations.

Employees must not copy confidential or restricted information to personal cloud-storage accounts, removable media, or unmanaged devices without explicit authorization.

8. Reporting Exposure

Employees who accidentally disclose, transmit, or expose confidential or restricted information must report the incident promptly to the IT security team.

Suspected unauthorized access to sensitive information must also be reported.

9. Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for understanding the classification of information they access and applying the appropriate handling requirements.

When classification is unclear, employees should consult the information owner or IT security team before sharing the information.

10. Compliance

Failure to follow information-classification and handling requirements may result in removal of access privileges and further action under applicable company policies.
