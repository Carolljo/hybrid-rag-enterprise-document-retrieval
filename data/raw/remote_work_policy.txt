REMOTE WORK POLICY

Purpose

This policy defines security, communication, and operational requirements for employees working remotely.

1. Remote Access

Employees accessing internal company systems from outside company facilities must use the company-approved virtual private network (VPN).

Restricted internal systems must not be accessed through public or unapproved networks without an active corporate VPN connection.

Employees must not share VPN credentials, authentication tokens, or company account passwords with another person.

2. Device Security

Remote work must be performed using company-managed devices unless explicit approval has been granted for another device.

Employees must keep operating systems, security patches, antivirus software, and endpoint-security tools up to date.

Endpoint-security software must not be disabled while accessing company resources.

3. Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for supported company systems.

Employees are responsible for protecting authentication codes, security tokens, and recovery credentials.

Suspicious authentication requests must be reported to the IT security team.

4. Public Networks

Employees should avoid accessing sensitive company information over unsecured public Wi-Fi.

When public Wi-Fi must be used, employees must connect through the approved corporate VPN before accessing internal systems.

5. Confidential Information

Confidential and restricted company information must not be displayed where unauthorized individuals can view it.

Employees working from shared or public locations must take reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized access to company information.

Restricted information must not be downloaded to personal or unmanaged devices.

6. Communication

Employees working remotely must remain reachable through approved company communication channels during their agreed working hours.

Sensitive company information must only be transmitted through approved communication and file-sharing systems.

7. Security Incidents

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported immediately to the IT security team.

Suspected credential theft, unauthorized access, malware, or accidental exposure of restricted information must also be reported promptly.

8. Compliance

Failure to follow remote-work security requirements may result in suspension of remote access privileges and further action under applicable company policies.
