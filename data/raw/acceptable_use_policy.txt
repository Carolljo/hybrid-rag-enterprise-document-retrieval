ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

Purpose

This policy defines acceptable and prohibited use of company devices, networks, accounts, applications, and technology resources.

1. Authorized Use

Company technology resources are primarily provided for legitimate business activities.

Employees must use company systems responsibly and in accordance with applicable security, privacy, and workplace policies.

Limited personal use may be permitted when it does not interfere with work responsibilities, create security risks, or violate company policies.

2. Company Devices

Employees must take reasonable precautions to protect company laptops, mobile devices, and other equipment from loss, theft, or unauthorized access.

Company-managed security software, antivirus tools, endpoint protection, and device-management controls must not be disabled or bypassed.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported immediately.

3. User Accounts

Employees must use only accounts and access permissions assigned or authorized for them.

Passwords, authentication tokens, and multi-factor authentication codes must not be shared with another person.

Employees must not attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems, accounts, files, or information.

4. Internet and Network Use

Company networks must not be used for unlawful activities, malicious activity, unauthorized scanning, or attempts to bypass security controls.

When accessing restricted internal systems remotely, employees must follow approved remote-access requirements, including use of the corporate VPN where required.

5. Software Installation

Employees must not install unauthorized software on company-managed devices.

Software required for business purposes should be obtained through approved company processes or authorized software repositories.

6. Email and Messaging

Company communication systems must be used responsibly.

Employees must exercise caution with unexpected links, attachments, authentication requests, and messages requesting sensitive information.

Suspected phishing messages should be reported through approved security-reporting channels.

7. Company Information

Confidential and restricted company information must only be accessed, stored, or transmitted using approved systems.

Employees must not upload sensitive company information to personal cloud-storage services, personal email accounts, or unauthorized external applications.

8. Removable Media

Use of removable storage devices may be restricted.

Employees must not copy confidential or restricted information to unauthorized removable media.

9. Monitoring

Company technology resources may be logged or monitored where permitted by applicable law and company policy for security, operational, and compliance purposes.

Employees should not assume that activity performed using company resources is private.

10. Security Incidents

Suspected malware, credential compromise, unauthorized access, lost devices, or accidental exposure of sensitive information must be reported promptly to the IT security team.

11. Prohibited Activities

Employees must not use company technology resources to distribute malware, intentionally damage systems, circumvent security controls, perform unauthorized access, or conduct activities prohibited by law or company policy.

12. Compliance

Violations of this policy may result in restriction or removal of technology access and further action under applicable company policies.
