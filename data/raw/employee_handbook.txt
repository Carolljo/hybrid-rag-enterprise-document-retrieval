NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version 2.1

1. WORKING HOURS

Standard working hours are Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Employees are expected to complete 40 working hours per week. Flexible working arrangements may be approved by the employee's manager based on team requirements.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave requests should normally be submitted at least seven days in advance through the employee portal. Leave requests are subject to manager approval based on operational requirements.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the following calendar year up to a maximum of five days.

3. SICK LEAVE

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Employees who are absent for more than three consecutive working days may be required to provide appropriate medical documentation.

4. REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week with manager approval. Employees working remotely must remain available during normal working hours and must comply with company information-security requirements.

Employees handling restricted information must use the approved corporate VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.

5. PROBATION PERIOD

New employees are normally subject to a six-month probation period. Performance and suitability for continued employment are reviewed before the end of the probation period.

6. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Full-time employees may receive up to $1,500 per calendar year for approved professional development activities, including technical certifications, training programs, and job-related courses. Expenses require manager approval before enrollment.

7. CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees must maintain professional behavior and protect confidential company and customer information. Harassment, discrimination, deliberate misuse of company systems, and unauthorized disclosure of confidential information may result in disciplinary action.
